[ FAQ ]

Questions,
answered.

What runs inside my workspace?

A Linux machine (Ubuntu 26.04) with T3 Code, VS Code in the browser, Claude Code, Codex and Oh My Pi, plus git, the GitHub CLI, Node 24, Python, uv and ripgrep. Your files live in /workspace.

Where is it hosted?

On servers run by AgentBox, reached through Cloudflare. Every workspace gets its own hostname under agentbox.io.vn, one per tool, so workspaces never share a browser origin with each other or with the dashboard.

What happens when I stop working?

An idle workspace goes to sleep and stops using runtime. Open its address and it wakes up again. Your files stay on disk the whole time.

How is it kept isolated?

Each workspace is a rootless Podman container under its own Linux user, with a read-only system, every capability dropped, hard CPU, memory, process and disk limits, and a firewall that blocks private networks, VPNs, cloud metadata and other workspaces. The security section shows how.

How does billing work?

Plans include a monthly allowance. Beyond it, runtime, CPU and memory are metered and drawn from your credit balance. The pricing above is read from the live plans.

How do I get access?

AgentBox is invite-only for now. Request access, and your workspace is created once an administrator approves your account.

What about my data and privacy?

Your files stay in your workspace, readable only by its user, and you can revoke signed-in sessions from your settings. This page sets no cookies and runs no analytics.
